💡 Key Non-Financial Strategic Considerations
  • Technology Risk: Leasing shields against rapid equipment obsolescence.
  • Working Capital: Buying requires cash up front; leasing preserves liquidity for daily operations.
  • Maintenance Burden: Asset ownership under Buy options places full repair risks on the business.
