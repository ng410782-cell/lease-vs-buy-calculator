# Equipment Capital Budgeting & Ind AS 116 Lease vs. Buy Decision Engine

An interactive capital budgeting utility designed to evaluate equipment acquisition choices (Purchase vs. Lease) across **SME (AS 19)** and **Corporate (Ind AS 116 / IFRS 16)** accounting frameworks. 

The engine incorporates direct financial incentives (government capital subsidies) and indirect operational factors (maintenance costs, residual salvage value, and technology obsolescence risk).

---

## Core Evaluator Features

1. **Dual Entity Mode (SME vs. Large Corporate):**
   * **SME Mode:** Focuses on cash flow preservation, local GAAP (AS 19), and government grant utilization.
   * **Corporate Mode:** Incorporates Ind AS 116 Right-of-Use (ROU) asset principles, tax shields, and debt capacity management.

2. **Capital Subsidy Integration:**
   * Factors in state/central capital incentives (15% to 35% subsidies for MSMEs, regional setups, and women-led enterprises) to dynamically adjust initial capital outlays.

3. **Direct & Indirect Operational Factors:**
   * **Residual Salvage Value:** Accounts for retained equipment equity at the end of useful life.
   * **Maintenance & Insurance (O&M):** Incorporates tax-deductible upkeep costs under direct ownership versus operating lease structures.
   * **Technology Obsolescence Risk:** Dynamically adjusts discount rates (WACC) based on asset lifecycles.
